# About Richard

Surf brings together two things I care deeply about: helping people learn, and making
powerful technology available to more people.

I created Surf because AI can dramatically expand what one person is able to do. I want
anyone who is willing and ready to learn to have the chance to experience that — and to
have fun doing it.

[Read why I created Surf →](why-surf.md)

## Biography

Richard Ng is a founder and educator who has worked with over 1,000 learners across AI
and software engineering.

Richard trained as a mathematics teacher, moved into software engineering, and was an
early hire at Multiverse (software engineering coach #2). Across a decade in education
and edtech, he has built global training programmes delivered across four continents,
including programmes used by Starling Bank, Beamery and Ocado, as well as startups backed
by Sequoia, Index Ventures and Y Combinator.

Richard graduated from the University of Oxford and is CEO of
[Native](https://withnative.ai).
