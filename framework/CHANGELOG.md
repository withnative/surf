# Surf working framework changelog

Surf currently has one working framework, `0.1.0`. Until a real production practice depends on it, the framework may be revised in place. Git history and review records preserve the development record; entries here are landmarks, not compatibility promises.

## Deterministic practice locator — 13 August 2026

- Adds one user-level locator for returning to a confirmed movable practice from another
  launch directory.
- Gives a valid launch-directory practice precedence and otherwise permits only the exact
  platform locator read before person-approved bounded recovery.
- Defines the two-field locator schema, fail-closed outcomes, combined setup consent,
  idempotent safe replacement and move, delete, backup and sandbox-denial behaviour.
- Keeps the locator and all practice content out of Surf MCP calls.

## Quickstart and reference architecture — 13 August 2026

- Recasts quickstart as a compact product spine and top-level router.
- Distinguishes moment guides from cross-cutting references and reserves “resource” for the MCP protocol primitive.
- Adds dedicated Shared map of development and Context and local practice references.
- Uses five moment guides: Setting up, Returning and capture, Evidence review, Intensive foundation, and Teaching and practice.
- Keeps Capabilities, Supporting literacies, and Builds as focused development references.
- Replaces the earlier release catalogue and migration machinery with one honest pre-production `0.1.0` surface.
- Makes focused retrieval a default while leaving agents discretion to consult relevant references.
- Treats fresh-client evaluation as indicative product evidence rather than a mechanical release gate.

## Multi-gear and curriculum exploration — 12–13 August 2026

The earlier multi-gear candidate established the substantive three-layer development model, direct intensive and personalised routes, explicit teaching and practice, protected capture, and evidence-bearing builds. Its useful content feeds the current framework; its numbered draft releases and migration scheme are not retained as a public compatibility catalogue.

## First learning-loop tranche — 11 August 2026

The first end-to-end development tranche established local inspectable context, a no-takeover learning role, protected capture, evidence review, plain-language interaction, and a public stateless briefing server. These remain source material for the working framework rather than immutable historical releases.
